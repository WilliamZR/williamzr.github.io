I am a first-year PhD student at The University of Hong Kong, advised by [Prof. Lingpeng Kong](https://ikekonglp.github.io/). My research focuses on diffusion language models.

I received my Bachelor's degree from the Integrated Science Program, Yuanpei College, Peking University, where I worked with [Prof. Chao Tang](http://cqb.pku.edu.cn/tanglab/index.htm) on metabolic flux analysis and single-cell foundation models. I earned my Master's degree from the Wangxuan Institute of Computer Technology, Peking University.
